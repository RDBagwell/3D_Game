# Emberwake: the story

One page: the premise, the cast, the beats and the ending. Everything here
is built as data in `src/game/data/` (dialogues, quests, items, enemies and
the two areas).

## Where and when

The same island chain as Island RPG: small islands, rowing boats, gulls,
and the **ember wisps**, little drifting lights that leave **Ember Cores**
behind, which islanders burn as lamp-stones. This story is set on an island
the RPG never visits, **Cinder Isle**, a day's row east of Gull Isle. It
stands on its own: nothing here needs the RPG, and RPG players get a few
nods (Wren, the lamp-stones, Tomas's boats).

## Premise

Every ember wisp in the islands is born in one place: the **Hearth**, an old
fire deep in the hill above Cinder Cove, kept burning by the villagers for
as long as anyone remembers. A month ago it went out. The wisps are fading,
the lamp-stones are going dark across the islands, and the stairs down to
the Hearth Halls are full of ash-bound bones that walk.

The player is a **courier** (you name them; the default is Ren), rowing
lamp-stone crates between islands. You land at Cinder Cove with a crate
nobody can light.

## Cast

| Who | Where | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Elder Ina** | By the Hearth gate | Keeper of the Hearth. Gives the main quest and opens the gate. Calm, dry, worried. |
| **Bram** | His forge by the square | Smith and shopkeeper. Sells tonics. Gruff, kind. |
| **Wren** | On the shore | A wisp scholar from Gull Isle (the RPG's Wren), here to find out why the wisps are fading. Lost her satchel of notes. |
| **Dorran** | The training ring | Retired gate guard. Runs the ring trial; thinks you're too soft. |
| **The Cinder Warden** | The Hearth chamber | An old ash-and-bone guardian built to tend the Hearth. With the fire gone it has turned to guarding the cold, and it won't let anyone near. |

Enemies are **ash-bound**: bones held together by the cold ash of the
Hearth. **Grunts** with swords and shields, **ash adepts** who fling cinders
from a distance, and **cindermites**, small fast things that come in packs.

## Beats (about 10 to 15 minutes)

1. **Arrival.** You step off the boat at the dock. Ina finds you at the
   Hearth gate and explains: the fire is out, the wisps are fading, and the
   halls below are crawling. Nobody here can fight. She opens the gate for
   you. *(Main quest: "The Cold Hearth".)*
2. **The village (optional, short).** Bram sells tonics. Wren lost her
   satchel on the rocks behind the houses; finding it earns her **Vigor
   Charm** (more health). Dorran's ring trial (beat the creatures he lets
   out) earns his old **Tempered Blade** (a stronger sword). The training
   dummy is by the ring.
3. **The Hearth Halls.** A hearthstone at the entrance marks a checkpoint.
   * **The Ash Steps:** a pack of cindermites.
   * **The Switch Hall:** a gate bars the way. A crystal switch across the
     hall, guarded by an ash adept, opens it when struck with the sword.
   * **The Key Vault:** two grunts guard a chest with the **Hearth Key**,
     which opens the locked door to the antechamber.
   * **The antechamber:** a second hearthstone, the last checkpoint.
4. **The Cinder Warden.** Phase one: wide sweeps and an overhead slam that
   leaves its blade stuck in the floor (the opening). Phase two, at half
   health: it calls cindermites and throws cinders. Beaten, it crumbles and
   leaves the **Hearth Ember**.
5. **Ending.** You set the Hearth Ember in the cold Hearth. It catches. Ina
   arrives, the wisps rise from the fire, and every lamp-stone in your crate
   lights at once. Credits. You can keep playing: the village celebrates,
   and everyone has something new to say.

## Tone

Island RPG's: warm, a little funny, never grim. People are kind; the threat
is a broken thing doing its job wrongly, not a villain. Dying sends you back
to the last hearthstone with everything you carried.

## Why this scope

One village and one short dungeon, three side activities, one boss: small
enough to finish and polish, big enough to show dialogue, quests, an
inventory, a shop, a puzzle, three enemy types, a boss and saves working
together.
